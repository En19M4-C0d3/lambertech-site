## Where this comes from

For eight years I worked as a Senior Teaching Assistant and Specialist Teacher, supporting SEND students in a school setting. It was demanding work, and it taught me things that don't show up on a typical IT CV: how to manage a caseload under pressure, how to communicate the same idea to five different audiences in five different ways, how to stay calm when a situation escalates fast, and how to hold to a process (safeguarding, GDPR, documentation) even when it's inconvenient.

A few years ago I started deliberately pivoting that experience toward IT and security. Not by going back to university, but by stacking certifications (CompTIA A+, Network+, Security+, and now CySA+), building a home lab, and taking on practical infrastructure work wherever I could get my hands on it — including a 200+ host network segmentation project at St Stephens Church, which is one of the first case studies I'll be publishing here.

## Why build this instead of just applying

I could have just kept updating a CV and sending it into the void. I chose to build this site instead for a few reasons:

**It's proof, not just a claim.** Anyone can write "strong troubleshooting skills" on a CV. A public write-up of an actual server migration, complete with the mistakes I made and how I diagnosed them, is a different kind of evidence — it shows how I actually think under a real problem, not just that I know the vocabulary.

**Documentation is the job, not separate from it.** Security and IT work lives and dies by good documentation — incident reports, runbooks, change logs. Practicing that habit publicly, on my own projects, is direct practice for exactly what the job actually looks like day to day.

**It forces me to actually finish things.** A home lab experiment that never gets written up is easy to abandon quietly. Knowing I'm going to document it properly is a genuinely useful pressure to carry projects through to a real conclusion.

## What to expect here

This site will hold two kinds of content going forward:

- **Project write-ups** — real infrastructure and security projects, including this site's own migration off a LAMP stack, my home lab builds, and past network projects — problem, approach, what went wrong, and outcome.
- **Blog posts** — shorter, more reflective pieces on the journey itself: what I'm studying, what I'm finding difficult, what a particular certification actually involved in practice.

The aim is simple: build a body of visible, honest evidence of the pivot from classroom to console, for anyone considering me for a role — and for myself, as a record of how far the journey's actually come.
